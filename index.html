📚 Quick Strategy Guide

✅ When to Call

  • • Your equity ≥ pot odds
  • • Good implied odds (deep stacks)
  • • Strong draws with position

📈 When to Raise

  • • Equity significantly above pot odds
  • • Value with strong made hands
  • • Semi-bluff with draws + fold equity

❌ When to Fold

  • • Equity well below pot odds
  • • No draw potential
  • • Facing aggression with weak hand

🧠 Key Concepts

  • • Position is power (BTN > CO > MP > UTG)
  • • Pot odds = call / (pot + call)
  • • Rule of 2&4: outs × 2 (turn), outs × 4 (flop)
